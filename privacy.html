Noggle Privacy Policy

This policy applies to the public launch version of Noggle provided by K Square Gaming Studio (“we”, “us”). For privacy questions, contact k2gamingstudio@gmail.com.

Guest profiles and leaderboards

Noggle uses Unity Gaming Services Authentication, Leaderboards and Cloud Code. A guest profile receives a persistent player identifier; an email address and password are not required. We process your chosen nickname, scores, ranking information and round statistics to operate leaderboards and validate scores. Round information includes combo statistics, board clears, bonus-time usage, completion time, game version, round identifiers and score-validation data.

Your nickname, score and other ranking information displayed on a leaderboard are public. Choose a fictional nickname without real names, contact details or sensitive information. Guest profiles and associated gameplay records are not anonymous simply because you do not provide an email address.

Local information and technical data

The game stores settings, tutorial state, nickname, personal best and pending score submissions on your device. Pending scores can upload when connectivity returns. Unity processes identifiers and technical connection information, including IP addresses, to operate its services.

Unity Analytics

We use Unity Analytics to understand game usage and improve Noggle. Analytics processes installation or player identifiers, approximate location and technical information, alongside session and gameplay events. We measure tutorial progression, rounds and bonus-time usage. We do not include nicknames, support email addresses or free-text messages in custom analytics events.

Optional gameplay analytics begins after you allow it. You can withdraw that choice through Settings → Privacy Choices without losing normal gameplay. Stopping collection does not itself erase earlier analytics records. Contact us or use Delete Account and Data to request deletion of associated analytics data. We use Unity’s deletion mechanisms to submit these requests; provider processing may continue after the game account is removed.

Google AdMob advertising

Noggle displays banner, interstitial and optional rewarded advertisements through Google AdMob. Google’s advertising SDK processes IP addresses, which can indicate general location; advertising and other device identifiers; ad or app interactions; and diagnostic information. These support ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. Advertising identifiers and other transmitted data depend on device settings, applicable privacy choices and the ad mode.

Where required, we request your advertising consent before requesting ads. Settings → Privacy Choices provides access to applicable advertising choices. Depending on your choices and region, ads may be personalized, non-personalized or limited. Non-personalized ads can still process information for delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. Android also provides controls to reset or delete your advertising identifier. Choosing not to allow personalization does not remove all advertisements.

Support and recipients

If you contact us, we receive your email address and information you provide. Support correspondence is handled through Gmail. Unity and Google process information to provide the services described above, and public leaderboard information is visible to other players. We may disclose information when required by law or to address security, fraud or abuse.

Read Unity’s Privacy Policy, Google’s Privacy Policy and Google’s information about partner services for additional provider information. Providers may process information outside your country, including in countries with different privacy protections.

Retention and deletion

We keep guest profiles and associated live leaderboard records while needed to provide the profile and rankings, until deleted or otherwise removed. Scheduled leaderboard resets do not delete the guest account. Our account-deletion flow removes the Authentication profile and its live leaderboard entries. We do not enable leaderboard archives for Noggle’s current leaderboard setup.

Open Settings → Delete Account and Data to initiate deletion. The app pauses gameplay and score uploads while a deletion request is pending. If offline, reconnect and retry; the request persists across app restarts. Successful in-app deletion also clears covered local game saves. A new guest profile is created only if you choose to start one.

Uninstalling the app does not delete online records. Reset Progress is separate from account deletion. For an external request, follow our account-deletion instructions. We aim to complete email requests within 30 days after verifying ownership, subject to applicable legal deadlines, and explain any need for additional information or time.

Resolved support correspondence is deleted 12 months after closure, except where an ongoing dispute or legal obligation requires longer retention. Limited technical, security or legal records can remain under applicable provider retention rules. Unity Cloud Code documents retention of personal data for up to 30 days. AdMob and Analytics records have separate provider retention and deletion processes; deleting a Noggle guest account does not guarantee immediate erasure of every provider-held record.

Your rights and choices

Contact us to request access, correction or deletion, or help with privacy choices. Depending on your jurisdiction, you may also have rights to portability, restriction, objection, withdrawal of consent or a complaint to a privacy regulator. We may need reasonable information to verify ownership. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing already lawfully performed.

Children

Noggle is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action. Additional protections for teenage players and consent requirements apply where required by local law.

Security and changes

We use secure transmission and restricted access to handle player information. No system is completely secure. We update this policy when our practices change and provide notice where required.
