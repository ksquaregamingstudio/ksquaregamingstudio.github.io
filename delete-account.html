Delete your Noggle account and data

Noggle is provided by K Square Gaming Studio.

From the app

Open Settings → Delete Account and Data and follow the confirmation. Stay online until completion. If the request is interrupted, reopen the app and select Retry Deletion.

Request deletion by email

Email k2gamingstudio@gmail.com with the subject Noggle Account Deletion. Explain that you want your Noggle guest account and associated data deleted. Include your player identifier or support code if you have it. If you do not, contact us for help locating the profile. A nickname alone is not proof of ownership. Do not send passwords, authentication tokens or identity documents unless a justified verification process specifically requires them.

We may ask for reasonable information to verify that the profile belongs to you. We aim to complete verified email requests within 30 days and notify you if additional information or time is needed, subject to applicable legal deadlines.

What is removed

Deletion removes the guest Authentication profile and its live leaderboard records. Successful deletion through the app clears covered local game saves. We also submit deletion requests for associated Unity Analytics data. An email-only request cannot directly erase files on your device; we will explain any local cleanup needed.

Limited provider logs and records retained for security or legal obligations may remain. See our Privacy Policy for retention details, including support correspondence and provider data. Account deletion is permanent. Continuing to play creates a new guest profile only when you choose to do so.
